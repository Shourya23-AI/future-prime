# Future Prime Owner Review Plan

## What we understood from the basic requirement
The application should centralize the work currently done manually in Word and Excel by:
- creating quotations and proposals
- tracking spare parts inventory
- managing orders, inbound import shipments, warehouse receipt, and outbound local deliveries
- storing service and warranty records
- recording technician travel and expenses
- supporting multiple user roles for sales, front desk, inventory, and service

## What will be developed
### Business value
- One system for all three companies: `Prime Associates`, `Parina International`, and `Prime Motors`
- Real-time inventory visibility
- Faster quote creation and approval
- Better tracking of inbound import shipments, warehouse receipt, and outbound delivery status
- Service tracking for free service schedules and warranties
- Technician attendance and expense recording

### Important features
- Customer and product master data
- Quote generation and approval
- Inventory receipt, rack assignment, stock balance, and low-stock alerts
- Service and warranty tracking
- Technician assignment and expense tracking
- Inbound import, warehouse receipt, outbound delivery, and customs clearance workflow

## Deployment summary
- The system will be deployed in the cloud so remote staff can access it from anywhere
- Data will be centrally stored and backed up
- Users will log in securely via a web/mobile application

## Why this approach is useful
- Eliminates manual Word and Excel work
- Helps sales and inventory staff work faster
- Provides a single source of truth for orders, stock, and services
- Makes product and spare parts information available to remote users
